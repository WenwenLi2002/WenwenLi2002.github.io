Bayesian Optimization on Networks

Wenwen Li, Daniel Sanz-Alonso, Ruiyi Yang

Choosing informative evaluations with Gaussian processes adapted to network geometry.

We develop Bayesian optimization on metric graphs using Whittle–Matérn Gaussian processes. Our analysis treats unknown objective smoothness and finite element approximations of the prior, with experiments on benchmark functions and source-location inference.

Gaussian processes · Bayesian optimization · Metric graphs

A closer look at the experiment

In this source-location experiment, Gaussian process Thompson sampling with the graph-based prior reaches the prescribed regret tolerance in 96% of trials, compared with 14% for the Euclidean prior. This comparison illustrates the value of respecting the domain’s geometry in this setting.

Elliptic Bayesian Inverse Problems on Metric Graphs

David Bolin, Wenwen Li, Daniel Sanz-Alonso

Recovering diffusion coefficients from noisy observations on networks, with posterior uncertainty.

We formulate Bayesian inverse problems for elliptic and fractional elliptic equations on metric graphs. Forward-model stability and Whittle–Matérn priors yield Bayesian well-posedness; finite element solvers and pCN MCMC support numerical reconstruction and uncertainty quantification.

Log-conductivity on a letter-shaped metric graph: the true field and its posterior mean inferred from noisy solution observations.

Bayesian inverse problems · Uncertainty quantification · PDEs on networks

Continuous Data Assimilation with Learned Surrogate Dynamics

Wenwen Li, Daniel Sanz-Alonso

Understanding state tracking with learned dynamics and partial observations.

We analyze continuous data assimilation with learned surrogate dynamics in a finite-dimensional setting. Tracking bounds separate surrogate approximation error from observation noise. We study learned vector fields and short-time solution maps, connecting training-data requirements with tracking guarantees.
