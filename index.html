Hi, I’m Wenwen. I’m a PhD student in Computational and Applied Mathematics at the University of Chicago, where I’m advised by Prof. Daniel Sanz-Alonso.

I’m interested in recovering unknown parameters and dynamics from data, and in quantifying the uncertainty in these estimates. My work combines mathematical analysis and computation to study Bayesian inverse problems and data assimilation. I’m also exploring Gaussian process methods on networks.
